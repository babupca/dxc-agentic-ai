# Connecting to VPN

Use the legacy OrbitVPN client for all remote access to internal systems.
1. Open OrbitVPN and choose your region.
2. Sign in with your Orbit account and your token code.
Error 809: reinstall the OrbitVPN client and restart your laptop.
VPN disconnects every 24 hours; reconnect to continue.
