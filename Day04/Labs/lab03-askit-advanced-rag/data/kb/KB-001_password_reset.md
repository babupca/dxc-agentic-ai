# Password Reset

Employees can reset their own password at https://reset.orbitcorp.example 24x7.
1. Enter your employee ID (format EMP-XXXX).
2. Approve the MFA prompt on your phone.
3. Choose a new password: minimum 14 characters, not one of your last 5 passwords.
After a reset, sign out of all devices and sign in again. Laptops must be connected to the office network or VPN to sync the new password.
The helpdesk can reset a password only after verifying identity via MFA or a video call with photo ID.
