# Password Reset

Employees can reset their own password at https://reset-old.orbitcorp.example (legacy portal) on weekdays from 8 AM to 8 PM.
1. Enter your employee ID.
2. Answer your three security questions.
3. Choose a new password: minimum 8 characters. Passwords expire every 90 days.
The helpdesk can reset a password after confirming your date of birth.
