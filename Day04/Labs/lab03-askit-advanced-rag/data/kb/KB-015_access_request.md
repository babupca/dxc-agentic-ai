# Requesting Access to Systems

Raise an Access Request naming the system, the role needed and a business reason.
Approval flow: your manager → the system owner. Typical time: 1–2 working days.
Access is reviewed every 6 months; unused access is removed after 90 days of inactivity.
The helpdesk cannot grant access without both approvals, even for urgent requests.
