# Lost or Stolen Device

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone immediately — call the helpdesk 24x7. This is always priority Critical.
The helpdesk will lock and remotely wipe the device and reset your password.
Also file a police report within 24 hours if stolen and send the copy to security@orbitcorp.example.
