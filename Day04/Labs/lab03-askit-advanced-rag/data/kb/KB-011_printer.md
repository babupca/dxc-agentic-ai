# Printing

Office printers use Follow-Me printing: print to "Orbit-Print", then tap your ID card at any printer.
Jobs are kept for 24 hours. Colour printing is enabled on request for Marketing and Design only.
Printer offline or jammed: report the printer number on its sticker in a ticket — priority Low.
