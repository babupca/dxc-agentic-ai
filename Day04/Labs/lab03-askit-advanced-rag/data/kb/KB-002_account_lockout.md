# Account Lockout

Accounts lock after 5 failed sign-in attempts and unlock automatically after 30 minutes.
Common cause: an old password saved on a phone (email app) or mapped drive retrying in the background.
Fix: update the password on every device, or remove and re-add the email account on your phone.
If you cannot wait 30 minutes, raise a ticket — the helpdesk can unlock after identity verification. Priority: High if it blocks client work.
