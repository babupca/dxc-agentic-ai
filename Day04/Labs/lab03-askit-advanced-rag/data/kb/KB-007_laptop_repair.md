# Laptop Not Working / Repair

First try: full shutdown (hold power 10 seconds), unplug dock, restart.
Blue screen, swollen battery, liquid damage or broken screen: stop using it and raise a Hardware ticket — priority High.
A loaner laptop is available same-day at metro offices while yours is repaired. Back up to OneDrive before handing it over.
