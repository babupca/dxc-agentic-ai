# Personal Devices for Employees

Employees may use a personal laptop on the VPN only after enrolling it in Orbit Mobile Management (Company Portal) and turning on disk encryption.
Personal phones may be used for Outlook and Orbit Authenticator after enrolment (see Email on Mobile).
Devices that are not enrolled are blocked from OrbitConnect automatically.
