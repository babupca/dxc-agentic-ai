# Ticket Priority and SLAs

| Priority | Meaning | First response | Target resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Critical | Security incident, or many users/whole site down | 15 min | 4 hours |
| High | One user fully blocked from work, or a VIP affected | 1 hour | 1 business day |
| Medium | Work possible with a workaround | 4 hours | 3 business days |
| Low | Questions, requests, minor issues | 1 business day | 5 business days |
A ticket's priority may be raised if a deadline is at risk (e.g. client meeting).
