# Email on Mobile

Use the Outlook mobile app only; built-in mail apps are blocked.
Enrol your phone in Orbit Mobile Management first (Company Portal app). Setup takes about 10 minutes.
Not syncing? Check the phone is compliant in Company Portal, then remove and re-add the account. Password changes require re-entering the password on the phone.
