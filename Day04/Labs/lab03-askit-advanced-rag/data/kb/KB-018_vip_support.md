# VIP Support

VIP users (CXOs, VPs and their assistants) get priority High by default and a named support engineer.
VIP tickets must be acknowledged by phone within 30 minutes during business hours.
Automated tools may gather information for VIP tickets but must hand over to a human before taking any action.
