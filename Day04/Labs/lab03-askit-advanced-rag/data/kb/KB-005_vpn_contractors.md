# VPN Policy for Contractors

Contractors receive VPN access only with an approved Access Request from their Orbit manager.
Contractor VPN access expires on the contract end date or after 90 days, whichever is earlier, and must be re-approved.
Contractors may only reach systems listed in their access request. Split tunnelling is disabled for contractors.
Personal devices are not allowed for contractors — only Orbit-issued laptops.
