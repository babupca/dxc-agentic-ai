# New Hire Laptop Setup

**Category:** Hardware
**ID:** KB-022

New joiners receive a pre-imaged Orbit laptop on day one. Setup takes about 45 minutes.
1. Sign in with the temporary password from your welcome email and set a new one.
2. Enrol the laptop in Orbit Mobile Management when prompted.
3. Install the standard apps from the Orbit Software Center.
If the laptop is not ready on day one, raise a Low priority ticket and your manager will be told.
